Follow your curiosity

Change the surface, choose your colors, and play with the sliders. Let it flow adds movement; Glass & fine details controls chromatic dispersion, glass depth, exposure, grain, and softness. For the sharp rainbow edges in the references, keep softness low and dispersion high.
